Dennis Stadelman’s 3 Peace at Skipper’s Smokehouse June 26

By Scott Hopkins on June 23, 2026

Dennis Stadelman’s 3 Peace at Skipper’s Smokehouse June 26

CopE founder Dennis Stadelman brings his new trio Dennis Stadelman’s 3 Peace to Skipper’s Smokehouse in Tampa Friday, June 26. The evening opens with a solo acoustic performance by Stadelman, followed by singer-songwriters Tina and Adrian Praino. The night concludes with a full-band performance by 3 Peace featuring special guest Juanjamon from CopE and The Juanjamon Band on saxophone and keys.

3 Peace features guitarist and vocalist Dennis Stadelman, bassist John Hogan, and drummer Steve Holland. Together, the trio draws on decades of professional live performance experience across rock, blues, funk, and improvisational music, creating a sound built on deep groove, dynamic interplay, and spontaneous musical exploration.

John Hogan brings a long history in the New York music scene, where he performed for decades with multiple projects before relocating to Florida and becoming active in the Tampa Bay music community. Drummer Steve Holland has spent his life behind the kit, performing across Florida in rock, blues, and funk bands since his early teenage years, developing a versatile and powerful rhythmic foundation.

At the center of the project is Dennis Stadelman, known for his work as guitarist, vocalist, and songwriter with CopE and Sonic Stew. In 3 Peace, Stadelman focuses his musical approach into a rock and roll trio format that emphasizes interplay, improvisation, and organic live energy.
