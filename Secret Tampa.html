Witches, Wizards and Muggles™ are all invited to celebrate 25 years of the Harry Potter™ films at this enchanting two-day drone show.

We have a hard time believing it, but it’s already been 25 years of the Harry Potter™ film series! The captivating saga took over many of our childhoods, and now, you’ll get to experience it anew in the Tampa skies come August 28th and 29th. 

During the open-air spectacle, 1,200 drones will etch characters, places and moments from the films into the skyline. Complete with iconic music and dialogue from the beloved series, the ultimate tribute will only be in town for a couple dates. Don’t miss it:  

DroneArt Show: Harry Potter™ at Tampa Bay Tournament Sportsplex

28 August 2026 20:30 + more dates

Tampa Bay Tournament Sportsplex

From $39.90

A celebration of the timeless saga

Before thousands of drones recreate legendary moments from the nostalgic film series that stole our hearts, you’ll get to lounge around before the show. Food, drinks and themed merch will be available for purchase on site, so you can snag the perfect souvenir. And once the drone show begins, prepare to be awestruck: the franchise’s most iconic soundtrack and cinematic dialogue take over the Tampa skyline for a true celebration of the films. 

And to elevate the wonder, DroneArt Show: Harry Potter™ equips attendees with LED wristbands that flash in sync with the drones above — crafting a sense of connection like no other. 

DroneArt Show: Harry Potter™ will only be in Tampa for a couple nights, so don’t miss out on the pure nostalgia! 

📅 August 28 & 29

📍Tampa Bay Tournament Sportsplex

⏳ Doors open at 7:15 PM

 Show starts at 9:15 PM, lasting 60 minutes with an intermission

👤 No age requirement
