Pirates Roster Moves: New-Look Bullpen Takes Shape

The Pirates’ bullpen will look much different for Tuesday night’s game against the Brewers. Pittsburgh activated the trio of trade acquisitions made at the deadline; right-handers Luke Weaver, Kirby Yates and Lake Bachar.

Weaver was acquired from the Mets along with cash considerations for infield prospect Sammy Stafura. The nearly-33-year-old is in the midst of a strong season. In 42 appearances this year, Weaver is 2-1 with a 1.84 ERA and one save. Over his last 30 appearances dating back to May 1, he has only allowed one run in 32 innings.

The Pirates acquired Yates from the Angels for minor-league pitcher Kyle Robinson. The 39-year-old Yates totaled 31 appearances with Los Angeles to start the year and is 0-5 with a 3.95 ERA, three saves and 36 strikeouts in 27.1 innings.

Bachar was acquired from the Marlins for right-hander Brandan Bidois and a pair of prospects. The 31-year-old has logged 58.2 innings across 38 appearances/four starts and is 1-2 with a 3.84 ERA and a pair of saves.

Weaver will wear jersey No. 18, Yates will wear No. 54 and Bachar will wear No. 71.

To make room on the active roster, the Pirates optioned right-hander Noah Murdock and left-hander Brandon Eisert to Triple-A Indianapolis. Pittsburgh also designated Ron Marinaccio for assignment on Monday.

Murdock made three-scoreless relief appearances after he was called up from Indianapolis last week. Eisert is 2-2 with a 6.29 ERA in 32 appearances between the White Sox and Pirates this season. Marinaccio posted a 6.75 ERA in four appearances for Pittsburgh prior to getting DFA’d.
