How Accreditation Reflects Our Commitment to Excellence

By Candice VincentAugust 3, 2026news
Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 1:23
Instaread

At Sacramento Children’s Home, our mission has always been to provide responsive, comprehensive, and compassionate care that empowers children, youth, and families to overcome challenges and build brighter futures. We are proud to share that our recent accreditation from the Council on Accreditation (COA) reflects that ongoing commitment to excellence.

COA accreditation is awarded to organizations that meet nationally recognized standards for quality, accountability, and continuous improvement. Earning this accreditation required a thorough review of our programs, policies, and practices to ensure we are providing the highest level of care and support.

While accreditation is an important milestone, it represents something much bigger than a certificate. It reflects our commitment to delivering services that are safe, effective, and centered on the needs of those we serve. It also recognizes the dedication of our staff, whose compassion and professionalism make a meaningful difference in the lives of children and families every day.

As we look to the future, this achievement reinforces our vision of strengthening our programs, embracing best practices, and continuing to evolve to meet the changing needs of our community. We remain committed to providing hope, healing, and opportunity for every child and family who walks through our doors.

We are grateful to our staff, volunteers, partners, donors, and community members whose support makes this work possible. Together, we will continue building stronger families and brighter futures.
