Explore America’s Railroad History Through Music at Paul Pratt Memorial Library

July 13, 2026 Submitted By Hilary Jenison

Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 1:46
Instaread

History will come alive through music, storytelling, and song when musician, songwriter, and historian Jon Waterman brings his acclaimed program, “The Railroad in Song,” to the Paul Pratt Memorial Library on Tuesday, July 28, at 6 p.m.

The free program invites audiences on a musical journey through the history of America’s railroads, blending traditional and original songs with engaging storytelling and an accompanying slideshow. Along the way, Waterman explores the people and events that helped shape the nation’s railroad legacy—from the construction of the Transcontinental Railroad and the contributions of Chinese and Irish immigrants to the stories of legendary figures like Casey Jones and John Henry, historic train wrecks, the Great Migration, women who worked on the railroads, hoboes, heroes, and more.

“The Railroad in Song” is part of Waterman’s Live Music Making History Live series, which encourages audiences to discover and celebrate history through music. Since launching the series in 2018, Waterman has presented programs at libraries, museums, and historical societies across the country, combining his passion for American history with performances inspired by the people and stories that have shaped it.

Waterman holds master’s degrees in Popular Music History and Psychology, along with a bachelor’s degree in History. He performs regularly with Li Waterman as the folk duo Sultana and has earned praise from audiences and organizations nationwide for his engaging blend of music, history, and storytelling.

The program is supported in part by a grant from the Cohasset Cultural Council, a local agency supported by the Mass Cultural Council.

The event is free and open to the public. For anyone who enjoys history, folk music, or discovering the stories behind America’s railroads, this promises to be an entertaining and educational evening.
