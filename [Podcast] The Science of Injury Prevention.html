No matter if you’re an active athlete or you just enjoy living an active life, injuries are something you want to stay clear of. But are injuries a given part of both training and life, or can you train in a way that greatly reduces your injury risk?

In today’s episode, we discuss a new research review looking into how different types of training affect your injury risk.
