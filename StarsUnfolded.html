Some Lesser Known Facts About Bara Sapoko Ndiaye

  • Bara Sapoko Ndiaye spent his childhood living with his mother and brothers in a small village near Dakar.
  • He was hooked on football from his childhood. Whether at home, at school or during free time, the ball was always around.
  • He was primarily playing with his cousins and with the other kids of his neighbourhood.
  • He started out with Thiès and Senegal Elite Stars in Senegal, and later moved to the Gambinos Stars Africa academy in Gambia.
  • After spotting him during some training there, he was subjected to a 3 week trial and joined that academy in 2022.
  • With the help of Gambinos he was able to find a place to stay, food and schooling.
  • His mother remained in Senegal and for him that was not easy. The two speak on the telephone almost daily even today.
  • Gambinos Stars Africa signed a new cooperation agreement with Red&Gold Football, a partnership between the German Bundesliga club Bayern Munich and American side Los Angeles FC which seeks out promising young players all over the world in 2023.
  • The tie up later enabled Ndiaye to enter Europe. He impressed during his two-week visit to Munich in October 2024, where his academy side played a match against Bayern’s Under 19 outfit.
  • Then he had the opportunity to train with Bayern’s under 19 team and reserve team, as well as some time with the senior team through February/March 2025.
  • The following summer Grasshopper Club Zurich in Switzerland signed him on a 2-month trial to play a number of preseason friendlies with Celta Vigo and Bayern Munich.
  • He was slowed down by an injury about this time, leaving him out of a portion of the trial.
  • Ndiaye did not make a single appearance for Gambinos Stars in the West Coast Region Third Division League between the 2025 to 26 season.
  • Then in January 2026, he moved to Germany and Bayern Munich on loan from Gambinos for the remainder of the season.
  • The change was a big one for him. He missed the food from home and most of all he missed his family and friends.
  • He was on edge at his first training session with the senior team and had an injury to top it off. The dressing room wasn’t so bad, though.
  • A number of those who play there speak French, including his countryman Nicolas Jackson, as well as Michael Olise and Dayot Upamecano.
  • He and Upamecano now go out together for meals from time to time and Ndiaye reveres him as an older brother as their mothers are from the same town in Guinea Bissau.
  • He made his debut for the Bundesliga on 11 April 2026. He came on in the 84th minute of an away defeat for FC St Pauli, five goals to nil, to make him the 10th teenager to make his debut under Kompany.
  • In the 78th minute, he replaced Raphaël Guerreiro in the 78th minute, in a home match against VfB Stuttgart, which was won 4-2 by Bayern just over a week later on 19th April 2026.
  • That won also gave Bayern their 35th Bundesliga title, meaning that Ndiaye had collected a league medal in his second outing for the club.
  • His debut came on 25 April 2026 away to Mainz 05. Bayern would trail three goals to zero at the half but would come back to win 4 goals to 3 as Ndiaye played 77 minutes.
  • In May 2026, Senegal coach Pape Thiaw named him in the preliminary squad for the 2026 FIFA World Cup.
  • He also made the final 26 for the tournament and played in the Round of 32 when Senegal was beaten 3-2 in extra time by Belgium.
  • German media reports up to 2026 indicated that Bayern were nearing a permanent transfer from Gambinos Stars for a fee of less than 5 million euros.
