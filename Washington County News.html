Community effort helps clear the way for Spanish Trail Cultural Arts Center

By Ali Moreland Jul 27, 2026 0 Comments
Juanita Meyers (left) and Barbara Russell (right) sort through a collection of books during the July 11 community cleanup, as volunteers prepared the former Chipley High School campus for its next chapter as the Spanish Trail Cultural Arts Center. [CONTRIBUTED PHOTO]

Spanish Trail Playhouse (STP) is moving forward with plans to transform the former Chipley High School campus into the Spanish Trail Cultural Arts Center, following the organization’s recent acquisition of the property.

Following several months of negotiations with the Washington County School Board, Spanish Trail Playhouse, Inc. officially took ownership of the campus on July 7, 2026. The acquisition includes nearly five acres and six buildings, creating an opportunity to preserve the history of the property while developing a community focused space focused on arts, education, recreation, entrepreneurship, and fellowship.

The vision for the campus extends beyond a traditional performance space. STP plans for the Cultural Arts Center to serve as a community hub where residents of all ages can gather, learn, create, and connect. The future center is expected to provide space for arts education, community clubs, workshops, small businesses, recreational activities, performances, meetings, celebrations, and other programs designed to bring people together.

One of the first major steps in that transformation came during a campus workday on July 11, when 57 volunteers spent the day cleaning, organizing, and preparing the property for future use.

Volunteers worked from 8 a.m. until around 4 p.m., clearing buildings, removing outdated furniture and debris, organizing costumes and scripts, preparing the auditorium, and sorting items for the upcoming campus yard sale. The effort filled four 40-yard dumpsters, while usable furniture and materials were moved to the gymnasium for the Spanish Trail Playhouse yard sale scheduled for August 14 and 15.

“It was pretty much a full day,” said Spanish Trail Playhouse President Kevin Russell. “We had 57 people show up, and everybody worked hard.”

The volunteer effort included community members, local supporters, leaders, and students completing service hours. Russell said the turnout reflected the community’s enthusiasm for the future of the property.

“This past weekend was proof that big things happen when a community comes together,” Spanish Trail Playhouse shared on social media following the workday. “We may not have captured a photo of every person who showed up, but please know how grateful we are for each volunteer who gave their time, energy, and helping hands.”

This first cleanup day was an important early milestone in preparing the campus for its next purpose, with plans to hold another community workday in January. While significant renovations remain ahead, progress is already underway as Spanish Trail Playhouse begins turning the former school campus into a place designed to serve future generations.

The Spanish Trail Cultural Arts Center represents a vision of preserving the history of the old Chipley High School campus while creating new opportunities for creativity, education, recreation, and community connection for years to come.
